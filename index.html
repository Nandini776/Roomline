Feed in a listing's price, location and history — Roomline runs it through a random-forest model and tells you which of the three room types it's most likely to be.

Start predicting How the model works

THE ROUTE

Three stops from listing to label

  1. Enter the listing

    Location, price, minimum stay and availability — the same details a guest sees.

  2. The model runs

    A random forest, trained on real NYC listings, weighs every feature against thousands of past examples.

  3. Get your line

    A predicted room type, plus how confident the model is across all three possibilities.

THE LINES

Meet the three room types

4 · 5 · 6 — EXPRESS

Entire home/apt

The whole space, all to the guest. No sharing, no stops along the way.

B · D · F · M — LOCAL

Private room

A private room inside a shared home. Common areas are shared with the host.

L — SHUTTLE

Shared room

The room itself is shared. The most communal — and least common — line.

Built on the NYC Airbnb Open Data listings dataset · a scikit-learn pipeline served with FastAPI · read about the model
